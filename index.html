Who I am

01 — profile

I'm a penetration tester and vulnerability analyst based in Egypt, finishing a B.Sc. in Computer Networks and Cybersecurity at Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology.

Most of my work follows the same shape: scope it, enumerate quietly, find the flaw that actually matters, prove the impact without breaking production, and write it up so it gets fixed. I've delivered full-cycle web, network and Android assessments, produced CVSS-scored reports against OWASP Top 10 and PTES, and hardened firewalls, routers and segmentation for enterprise networks.

Testing is only half of it. I also build the detection side — my graduation project Observer is an IEEE-published IoT cyber-resilience framework pairing Suricata, FortiGate and an ELK/MISP pipeline with SOAR-driven recovery, reaching 86.71% detection recall across 34 attack scenarios.

What I care about is the part after the shell: clear evidence, honest severity, and remediation that still holds a quarter later.
